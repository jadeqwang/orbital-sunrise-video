On 18 March 1965 Alexei Leonov stepped out of Voskhod-2, whose name means sunrise, and became the first person in open space. His suit ballooned until he could not get back in. He bled his own air out to fit through the airlock, the automatic guidance failed, and he and Pavel Belyayev flew the re-entry by hand and came down in the snow of the Ural taiga.

Somewhere in the middle of it, with colored pencils tied to his wrist, Leonov drew the orbital sunrise. The drawing survived. Every frame of this film is drawn the same way: in colored pencil, by code, over reference footage that never appears on screen.
