# Permission request: Leonov's «Восход» ("Sunrise") drawing, 1965

Compiled 2026-10-01 for Jade Wang ("Orbital Sunrise" music video, plus the Austin "art made in
space" exhibition).

**Method note:** page fetches were blocked by the network egress proxy again (gctc.ru returned
`EGRESS_BLOCKED`). Everything below comes from **search-engine excerpts**, so I have not read
the pages themselves. Open each URL and check the contact details before you use them. Phone
numbers and emails change.

**Not legal advice.** Background on who owns what is in `RESEARCH_R4.md` §3. In short:
**Leonov's heirs** hold the copyright in the drawing, and the **Gagarin CTC museum** owns the
physical sheet and controls the photographs of it. You need permission from both.

## 1. Who to contact

### 1a. Leonov's heirs: Oksana Alekseevna Leonova (Оксана Алексеевна Леонова)

- **Family status.** Leonov's widow, **Svetlana Pavlovna Leonova, died on 6 Nov 2021**
  ([RG](https://rg.ru/2021/11/06/ushla-iz-zhizni-vdova-kosmonavta-alekseia-leonova.html),
  [RBC](https://www.rbc.ru/rbcfreenews/6186c26b9a7947b8d6fdbcad)). His elder daughter Viktoria
  also died before him
  ([b-port](https://b-port.com/news/332828)). That makes **Oksana (b. 1967) the most likely
  principal heir**. Viktoria's children, if she had any, could also be heirs. I have not confirmed
  this. Ask Oksana who holds the rights.
- **What she does.** She is a documentary and feature producer who looks after her father's
  legacy. She recorded his last long interview. That interview became the basis of *Алексей
  Леонов. Космос внутри* (2022, shown at STARMUS;
  [RG](https://rg.ru/2022/09/07/kosmos-vnutri.html),
  [1tv](https://www.1tv.ru/doc/pro-zhizn-zamechatelnyh-lyudey/aleksey-leonov-kosmos-vnutri-dokumentalnyy-film))
  and *Алексей Леонов. По самому краю* (2024, Кинокомпания «Гамма»; she is a co-producer; it won
  APKiT's best documentary award;
  [culture.ru](https://www.culture.ru/events/5693683/aleksei-leonov-po-samomu-krayu),
  [space-fest.ru](https://space-fest.ru/tproduct/557681306-449374908681-aleksei-leonov-po-samomu-krayu-alexey-le)).
  She worked on the 90th-anniversary programme (2024) and presents the films at regional
  screenings (Kemerovo, Novgorod, Kaluga, Perm). In July 2026 she visited the Voskhod 2 landing
  site ([Rodina](https://rodina-history.ru/2026/07/24/kak-kosmonavta-leonova-spasali-v-tajge-sekretnyj-arhiv-pokazali-ego-docheri.html)).
  She helped her father mount his first painting exhibition (2006)
  ([kp40.ru](https://www.kp40.ru/news/perekrestok/112153/)). Older profiles say she lived in Los
  Angeles and worked in film/video distribution
  ([spacefacts](https://www.spacefacts.de/family/cosmonauts/english/leonov_aleksei.htm),
  [360.ru](https://360.ru/tekst/interesnoe/kak-zhivet-semja-kosmonavta-leonova/)). Recent
  coverage places her mostly in Russia.
- **Public contact.** **I found no foundation, official website or verified social-media
  account** for her. Do not guess at addresses. The best routes, from most to least likely:
  1. **Through the museum or Roscosmos.** Ask the Gagarin CTC press service (1b) to pass your
     letter on to her. They work with the family on anniversaries.
  2. **Through the Museum of Cosmonautics, Moscow** (Музей космонавтики). It ran the 2024
     90th-anniversary programme and the exhibition «Судьба моя – я сам», and it shows his
     paintings ([kosmo-museum.ru](https://kosmo-museum.ru/news/muzey-kosmonavtiki-predstavil-vystavku-posvyaschyonnuyu-90-letiyu-alekseya-leonova)).
     Its own pages list the press service as **PRMK@culture.mos.ru**, +7 (499) 750-23-00
     ext. 1088 ([press office](https://kosmo-museum.ru/press_office)), and general mail as
     **museikosmonavtiki@culture.mos.ru** ([contacts](https://kosmo-museum.ru/contacts)).
  3. **Through the film's producer, Кинокомпания «Гамма» (Moscow).** I found its contact
     details only on business-directory aggregators, not on a company site, so I have not
     listed them. Do not confuse it with the St Petersburg firm «Гамма-Продакшн»
     (gamma-production.ru), which is a different company.
  4. **Through the Austin museum.** If it is borrowing other cosmonaut material, its curators may
     already know someone at the Memorial Museum of Cosmonautics or GCTC who can make the
     introduction.

  the drawing has left Russia. Press coverage credits images to *"Museum of the Yuri Gagarin

- **Science Photo Library.** It sells prints of Sokolov/Leonov space paintings via the ASAP

  ([example](https://fineartamerica.com/featured/painting-of-leonov-during-first-spacewalk-asokolov--aleonov-asap-science-photo-library.html?product=art-print)).
  That covers the painting reproductions, not this drawing, but SPL may know who has represented
  the estate.
- **Book publishers.** *In the Stream of Stars* (Workman, 1990, with Sokolov), *Life Among
  Stars*, *Star-Roads*, and Leonov's books published by AST
  ([ast.ru](https://ast.ru/news/kosmonavt-leonov-i-ego-knigi/)). Older editions predate the
  estate. A recent publisher may have dealt with the heirs.

  it represents Leonov. A one-line enquiry costs nothing.

## 2. Practical notes

- **Language.** Write in Russian with the English underneath. GCTC's formal correspondence is in
  Russian. Oksana is a trained military translator (English), so English works for her, but
  writing in Russian is a courtesy. **Have a native speaker check the Russian drafts below
  before sending.**
- **Who sends it.** Museum image rights are usually handled **registrar to registrar**, or by the
  rights-and-reproductions officer. The Austin museum's registrar should send the museum letter
  on letterhead, for the exhibition and the music video together. That also meets GCTC's
  "signed by the head of the organisation" rule. Jade should send the letter to Oksana herself,
  or co-sign it, because the request is personal.
- **What to include.** The work and its credit; the exact use (a few seconds in the video plus
  exhibition display, online or offline, territories, term, and that the use is non-commercial);
  what you are asking for (permission, a high-resolution file, the preferred credit line, and
  any fee); a link to or offer of the video; contact details; signature and letterhead.
- **Timing.** Allow **4–12 weeks**. State institutions tend to pass requests up through several
  levels of approval, and replies slow down around holidays (early January, 1–10 May). Send a
  polite reminder after about 3 weeks, and phone the press service if email gets no answer.
- **Have a fallback ready.** If no reply arrives in time, use the original-homage version from
  `RESEARCH_R4.md` §3 (Jade's own orbital-sunrise image with a spoken or text reference to
  Leonov's drawing and no reproduction of it).
- **Sanctions and payments (high level, not legal advice).**
  - The **Gagarin CTC appears on Ukraine's NSDC sanctions register**
    ([OpenSanctions](https://www.opensanctions.org/entities/NK-XnMRZJoeGpRGuAhzpwCaoe/)).
    I could not confirm from search excerpts whether **GCTC or Roscosmos itself** is on the
    **US OFAC SDN list** (several Roscosmos subsidiaries are). Before any agreement or payment,
    the Austin museum should **screen GCTC, Roscosmos and any payee** using
    [OFAC Sanctions List Search](https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov/).
  - US sanctions law has an exemption for **"information and informational materials"**
    (the Berman Amendment, IEEPA §1702(b)), which covers artworks and photographs. OFAC reads it
    narrowly where blocked persons are involved
    ([Ropes & Gray on the 2020 OFAC art advisory](https://ropesgray.com/en/newsroom/alerts/2020/11/OFAC-Issues-Art-Advisory-Highlighting-Sanctions-Risk-in-the-Art-Trade)).
    A **royalty-free, non-commercial permission** with no money changing hands carries the least
    risk. If a fee is requested, have the museum's counsel review it first. US banks may also
    refuse to send transfers to Russian banks.
  - The heirs are private individuals, so dealing with them is generally simpler, but the same
    screening and payment caveats apply if any money goes to a Russian bank.
  - The museum may decide it is better not to pay a Russian state body at all and to ask only for
    permission and a credit line. That is common, and the letters below are written that way.
